# 👻 Ghost Theremin

Wave at your webcam and play a haunted instrument. Every gesture snaps to a scale an LLM summons for you, so it is very hard to sound bad.

Built for **Kiroween 2026** (Frankenstein category) with [Kiro](https://kiro.dev).

## The chimera
| Part | Era | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Theremin | 1920s | Touchless instrument that inspired the interaction |
| MIDI | 1983 | Output to real synths and DAWs |
| Computer vision (MediaPipe) | today | Hand and head tracking in the browser |
| LLM | today | Summons the scale, tempo, and chords from a sentence |

## Who it's for
People who can't grip or press a traditional instrument, the music therapists and teachers who work with them, and anyone who wants to make music in 30 seconds.
